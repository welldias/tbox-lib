text-indent: hanging and each-line

40px: only the first line is indented, as before, and the other lines start at the left margin.

40px hanging: the first line stays at the margin and every following line is indented forty pixels.

30px each-line: the first line is indented
and the line after a forced break
is indented again too.

2em hanging each-line: combines both
behaviors in this paragraph with text long enough to wrap.

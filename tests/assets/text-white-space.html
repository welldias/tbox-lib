white-space, text-wrap, white-space-collapse and tab-size

pre: keeps spaces and newlines, never wraps at the width

pre-wrap: keeps spaces and newlines, and wraps at the width

pre-line: collapses spaces but keeps newlines

pre-wrap: the spaces at the end of the line hang outside the box

break-spaces: the spaces at the end of the line take room and wrap

text-wrap: nowrap keeps everything on a single line and clips

text-wrap-mode: nowrap has the same effect on long text

preserve: spaces kept and line breaks too

preserve-breaks: spaces collapse but line breaks stay

tab-size: 8 one two
